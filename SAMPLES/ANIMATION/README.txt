DRAW animation samples

bounce-ball.png              8 frames of a bouncing ball (32x32 each, 10 fps), frame data
                             saved in the PNG. It lands on frame 5. Num 0 = lightbox,
                             Num Enter = play.
bounce.gif                   The same 8 frames as an animated GIF (opens as frames).
ball-sequence/               The same 8 frames as ball_0001.png .. ball_0008.png
                             (ANIM > Import PNG Sequence...).
sheet-for-detect-frames.png  A sheet with 2 px gaps and no frame data (ANIM > Detect Frames).
bounce-tick.wav              A soft tick every 0.8 s - on the landing frame at 10 fps
                             (ANIM > Soundtrack...).
