#   FG / BG  - FG is the SOLID square, BG the OUTLINE square (as @fgbg: white FG on black BG).

#              (IMAGES/LAYERS/layer-btn-*: add +, delete x, up / down chevrons, group folder,
#              merge = sheets + down arrow).
#   cycling  - the @layer-color-cycle piece: a strip of three chips under a loop arrow.
